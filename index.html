THE STORY

The kingdom of Bellavista was powered by the Heart of the Pink Kingdom. It kept the flowers blooming and generally prevented everyone from becoming boring.

One morning it vanished. The sky went gray. The royal frog resigned. A sock was stolen.

The kingdom needs a hero. All the actual heroes are busy. So: you.

HOW TO PLAY

  • MOVE — arrow keys, WASD, or the D-pad
  • TALK / CONFIRM — SPACE, ENTER, Z, or A
  • RUN — hold SHIFT, or the B button
  • SEARCH — face a thing and press A
  • Gather 7 hearts, 5 stars and 3 magical flowers
  • Wander behind things. That is the whole trick.

WARNING

The kingdom is troubled by THE BEIGE ONES, creatures who remove colour and personality from things.

They are not dangerous. They are just very, very tiring.
