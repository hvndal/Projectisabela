THE STORY

The kingdom of Bellavista was powered by the Heart of the Pink Kingdom. It kept the flowers blooming and generally prevented everyone from becoming boring.

One morning it vanished. The sky went gray. The royal frog resigned. The kingdom needs a hero: you!

HOW TO PLAY

  • MOVE / RUN — arrow keys, WASD, or the D-pad
  • ACTION / TALK — SPACE, ENTER, Z, or A button
  • SPRINT / ATTACK — hold SHIFT or the B button
  • Collect all targets and secrets to complete the quest!

TIPS & SECRETS
